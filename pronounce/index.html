Pronounce My Name

How to pronounce "Ge Yu".

My name Ge Yu is pronounced roughly as "guh yoo" — closer to the "g" in go than the "g" in gem, and the "yu" rhymes with you.

In Mandarin (汉语拼音), it is written Gě Yú (with third-tone Gě and second-tone Yú).

Feel free to call me Ge for short.
